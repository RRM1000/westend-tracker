How to read this. Prices are the cheapest seat the box office advertises for that performance — the “from” price. They are not what every seat costs, and they move.

Where a performance shows limited availability, that means seats were unavailable when we looked. It does not necessarily mean they sold — houses hold seats back for the production, for comps, and for access bookings. Some venues (marked “dates only”) do not publish prices through the channel we read, so only dates and availability are shown.
